Transitioning from [[High Mount]] to [[S Mount]]

# Summary
The transition from [[High Mount]] to [[S Mount]] involves shifting your positioning to gain greater control and open up additional submission opportunities.
# Objectives
- **Optimize position:** Change from parallel to perpendicular relative to the bottom player, reducing their ability to escape.
- **Set up attacks:** Create a more advantageous angle for submissions by isolating one arm or exposing openings in the opponent's defense.
# Key Details
- **Knee movement:** From high mount, move one knee further "north" so it passes above the player's head (or as high as you can get it).
- **Weight distribution:** Keep your weight centered on the bottom palyer's upper torso to maintain stability during the transition.
- **Leaning:** Lean slightly over the "northern" knee, which allows you space and weight distribution to curl your other leg around the bottom player's head.
- **Rotation:** As you shift, rotate your body from being parallel to the botom player to becoming perpendicular, establishing the S mount position.
