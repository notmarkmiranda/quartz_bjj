Transitioning from [[Mount]] to [[High Mount]]
# Summary
The transition from mount to high mount can be used both offensively and defensively. It’s especially effective when the bottom player is focused on defending chokes, or when controlling a larger, more explosive opponent who may attempt to bridge.

# Objectives
• Create openings for upper body submissions (e.g., [[Cross Collar Choke]], [[Arm Bar]])
• Reduce the bottom player’s ability to bridge and escape
• Isolate and control the arms
# Key Details
1. **Establish a Crossface**
Begin by securing a solid crossface to control the head and posture. This limits their ability to turn and sets up potential choke attacks.

2. **Read Their Defense**
If the bottom player is overly focused on defending chokes by keeping their hands tight to their neck, they may leave space for you to climb into high mount.

3. **Use Forearm Frames**
Slide your forearms above the bottom player’s head to act as barriers. This prevents them from following your hips as you move forward and gives you leverage to stabilize your weight.

4. **Leverage with Feet**
Place your feet or toes on their hips briefly as a base to help drive your weight forward. This keeps you balanced and minimizes the risk of being bridged off during the transition.

5. **Progressively Isolate the Arms**
As you move into high mount, work to gather the bottom player’s elbows and bring them above the line of their shoulders. This severely limits their defensive posture and exposes their arms to attacks.

6. **Maintain Chest Pressure**
Keep your hips low and chest connected as you advance. Avoid floating too high without control of their upper body.
