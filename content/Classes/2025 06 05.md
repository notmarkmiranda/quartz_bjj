## Summary
This class focused on [[São Paulo pass]]ing from the top of [[Closed Guard]], transitioning into [[Half Guard]], and finishing with pressure-based passing options. The goal was to open the guard safely while controlling the bottom player’s hips and posture, then initiate an esgrima-style pass with strong upper body pressure.
## Objectives
- Learn how to safely open closed guard using São Paulo mechanics
- Control the bottom player’s posture and hips during the pass
- Transition smoothly from closed guard to half guard to passing position
- Apply pressure to flatten the opponent and initiate a pass (head-to-mat or turning pass)
## Key Points
- Control their belt and pinch your elbow to your side to protect your wrist
- Switch hips and drive your pressure low and away to break their guard tension
- Reach back to open the guard and trap their foot between your thighs to enter half guard
- Transition your belt grip to the scruff of their collar and begin the esgrima pass
- Keep them flat while sprawling your hips down to limit their movement
- Two finishing options:
    1. Head-to-mat finish: drive your head next to theirs, push their head away, and extract your leg
    2. Turning half pass: pivot and rotate your hips around for a directional half guard pass
