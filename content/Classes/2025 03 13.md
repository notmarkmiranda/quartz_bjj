Meat grinder from [[Omaplata]]
### Other notes from spreadsheet
The [[Meat Grinder]] is when you shoot an [[Omaplata]] and they person rolls to counter, keep the arm pinched between your thigh and stomach, come up off your butt and sit on their side. They may possibly roll you backwards, but you just rotate 1/4 turn and return to omaplata. From the meat grinder, you can also omaplata again, or move to mounted triangle. my 3 rounds were drilling with Wax, Sam, & Dean
