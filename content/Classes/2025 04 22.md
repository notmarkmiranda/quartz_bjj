## Summary
This was a class focused on replacing guard starting from the [[Half Guard|worst ever half guard]]. It went over steps to use a frame inside their knee on the open side, use your bicep punch at the side of their ear to get onto your side, replace knee shield, break the superhold by "make banana". Then transitioned to using a 4 finger in grip against their neck, combined with a stiff arm, wrestling up and 1) replacing guard, 2) granby roll then go to [[Sleeve & Collar]], or [[De La Riva]].
