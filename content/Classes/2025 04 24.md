## Summary
We worked on learning going from top [[Side Control]], then untucking the bottom player's lapel from their belt, and using it to chicken wing their arm and transferring it to your hand that is also crossfacing them. The finishes we learned from there were:
1. if they don't work to free their arm, just perform a [[Cross Collar Choke]] on them.
2. if they pull their arm backwards out of the loop, then slide your hand in the loop and [[Ezekiel Choke]] them.
3. if they push their arm all the way through, go for a [[Brabo Choke]]
