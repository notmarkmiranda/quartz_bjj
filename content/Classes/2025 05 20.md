## Summary
This class focused on continuing half guard passing, specifically dealing with a knee shield and transitioning to mount. The sequence emphasized control, pressure, and positional advancement.
## Objective
To effectively pass from top [[Half Guard|half guard]] by neutralizing the knee shield, establishing dominant upper body control, and transitioning to [[Mount|mount]] while limiting the bottom player’s mobility.
## Key Points
**Knee Shield Shuck:**
	•	Use a same-side collar grip and pant grip near the middle of the thigh.
	•	Push with the pant grip while pulling with the collar grip to flatten the opponent.
**Cross Face Control:**
	•	Ensure your elbow pit is tight on their neck, and your hand is deep near their armpit.
	•	Try to connect your elbow to your knee on the same side to create pressure and limit movement.
	**Underhook or Elbow Control:**
	•	Secure an underhook on the far side or control their elbow if the underhook isn’t available.
	**Passing to Mount:**
	•	Use your free foot to pin their thigh, then free your trapped leg.
	•	Once in mount, collect their far elbow and bring it above their head to establish control.
