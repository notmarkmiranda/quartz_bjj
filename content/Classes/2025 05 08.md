## Summary
This class was about what to do in the instance of a double guard pull. There were a lot of conversations about the nuances of points, advantages, etc.

The progression was from combat base
1. grabbing their extended foot lifting it up, and sliding underneath them to a "bad [[Single Leg X]]" or [[Modified X]].
2. grabbing their extended foot, transitioning to the mean part of your wrist onto their achilles, grabbing their other knee with your other hand, using this to roll so your ankle lock side is closer to the ground. in order to finish this, you would drop from your elbow to your shoulder, replacing the spot where your elbow was posted (going straight down), grabbing their pant in front of their shin, pushing away, then pulling your head back
In the event that you're in a position closer to 50/50, going to a [[De La Riva]] position and using this to enter into a [[Berimbolo]]. I don't fully understand this one.
