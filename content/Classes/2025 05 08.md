## Summary
This class focused on tactics from a **double guard pull** scenario, including positional progression, submission entries, and point-scoring nuances. The instruction emphasized how to convert a neutral guard exchange into an offensive opportunity, particularly entering into modified X-guard positions and attacking with leg locks or Berimbolo transitions.
## Objectives
• Understand common outcomes and scoring rules around double guard pulls.
• Learn to establish dominant leg entanglement positions from combat base.
• Develop an entry to single leg X / modified X from a seated exchange.
• Practice a transition into a straight ankle lock finish.
• Begin exploring De La Riva to Berimbolo as a follow-up when X-guard isn’t available.
## Key points
1. **Combat Base Entry to [[Modified X]] / Bad [[Single Leg X]]**
			• Opponent extends a foot → grip the ankle and elevate.
			• Slide underneath into a shallow X-guard variant.
2. **[[Straight Ankle Lock]] Setup from [[Modified X]]**
			• Wrist pressure on their achilles; other hand grabs their far knee.
			• Rotate/roll toward the ankle lock side, aiming to bring that leg to the floor.
			• Transition from posting on your elbow to dropping to your shoulder.
			• Secure their pant leg/shin with your top arm, push with your legs while posturing your head back for finish mechanics.
3. **If opponent’s position resembles 50/50**
			• Shift to a [[De La Riva]] hook.
			• Use this to initiate a [[Berimbolo]] entry (conceptually introduced but not fully drilled).
