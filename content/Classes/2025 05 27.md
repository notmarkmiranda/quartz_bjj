## Summary
In this session we refined our [[Half Guard|half guard]] passing sequence, focusing on beating the knee-shield and securing dominant upper-body control before finishing with either a [[Three Quarter Mount|three-quarter mount]] or a [[Knee Slice Pass|knee slice]].

## Overview
1. **Win the Hip-Knee Battle** – Slide your free (untrapped) knee across their belt line and post your outside hand far past the opponent’s head to collapse the shield. 
2. **Break the Elbow-Knee Frame** –
    - If there’s space: drive your knee downward to pin their lower leg.
    - If they’re tight: capture their forearm, elevate it, and insert your knee between their elbow and knee to pry the frame open.    
3. **Re-Center Your Weight** – Rock back so you’re not extended, settle a heavy cross-face, and secure the far-side under-hook.
4. **Finish the Pass** –
    - **Three-quarter-mount** by clearing your trapped foot toward the far side, or
    - **Near-side knee slice** if their shield collapses inward.
## Key Objectives
- Beat the knee-shield early by posting long and driving the knee to the mid-line.
- Disassemble the elbow-knee connection with controlled forearm isolation and knee insertion.
- Re-establish strong chest-to-shoulder pressure (cross-face) before advancing.
- Transition smoothly to either three-quarter-mount or a clean knee-slice, depending on the opponent’s reactions.
