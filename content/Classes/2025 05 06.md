## Summary
Today’s focus was on passing [[Half Guard]] by recognizing and exploiting the bottom player’s defensive structure. The overarching goal is to flatten the opponent and secure a strong cross face position, setting up dominant passing options.
## Objectives
• Identify common bottom player defenses in [[Half Guard]].
• Learn appropriate passing strategies based on the opponent’s shield position.
• Develop sensitivity to transitions, submissions, and counters during passing.
## Key Concepts & Techniques
1. **[[Weave Pass]]**:
• If the bottom player’s knee shield is positioned too low, thread your arm under their top leg (weave) and apply pressure to pass around it.
2. **[[Smash Pass]]**:
• If the shield is too high, stuff their bottom foot between your legs. Flatten them and drive forward pressure to initiate a smash pass.
3. **[[Knee Slice Pass|Knee slice]] Entry**:
• If the bottom player places their foot on your hip, sneak your inside arm to the far hip, then rotate into a knee slice position.
4. **[[Wrist Lock]] Opportunity** (from [[James Borders|Black Belt James]]):
• When their elbow is exposed, use your outer arm to pin it against their torso. With the free hand, apply a wrist lock for a surprise submission threat.
