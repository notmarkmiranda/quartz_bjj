## Summary
The top player is sitting on the torso of the bottom player, typically with knees on the mat and hips low. It's a dominant position for the top player
## Goals
### Top player goals:
- Maintain control and pressure
- Submit the bottom player (common: [[Arm Bar]], [[Americana]], [[Cross Collar Choke]], [[Ezekiel Choke]])
- Transition to stronger control (eg [[Mount to Back Mount]], [[Mount to High Mount]])

**Tactics:**
- Keep hips low, centered, and heavy
- Control the bottom player's hips to block escapes (eg. grapevines, knee pinching, floating pressure)
- Use crossface or underhooks to control upper body
### Bottom player disadvantages:
- Very limited movement
- Hard to attach or defend without creating space
- High risk of getting submitted

**Escape options:**
- Bridge and roll (upa): trap one side arm and leg and bridge & roll the top player over
- Elbow escape: Create space to recover half or full guard
