## Summary
High mount is a variation of the [[Mount]] position where the top player shifts their weight higher up on the bottom player's torso, often with knees near the armpits. This makes it harder for the bottom player to bridge and escape, and opens up strong submission opportunities for the top player.
## Goals
### Top player goals:
- Maintain control and pressure
- Submit the bottom player (common: [[Arm Bar]], [[Americana]], [[Cross Collar Choke]], [[Ezekiel Choke]])
- Transition to strong control positions (eg [[S Mount]])
**Tactics:**
- Keep hips low, centered, and heavy, but positioned higher on the torso
- Control the bottom player's arms by gathering them with your arms
- Isolate arms for submissions or bait reactions for transitions
### Bottom Player Disadvantages:
- Severely limited movement
- Hips are free, but no leverage to generate power
- Arms are vulnerable to isolation
- Very high risk of submission
**Escape Options:**
- Framing and shrimping: Create frames against the top player's hips or armpits, then try to shrimp your body and bring their hips back over yours, returning to standard mount.
