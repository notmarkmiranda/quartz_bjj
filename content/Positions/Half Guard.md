## Summary
Half guard is a ground position where the bottom player has control of one of the top player's legs using their own legs, usually trapping it between their thighs while the top player is trying to pass.

### Bottom Half Guard
**Goal:** Maintain control, prevent the pass, and work to either:
- Sweep
- Take the back
- Regain full guard
**Common Variations:**
- [[Z Guard]]
- [[Deep Half Guard]]
- [[Lockdown]]
**Risks:** You're halfway passed, frames and underhooks are your best defense.
**Terms:**
#### The Worst Bottom Half Guard
You've trapped their leg, but they've flattened you out and secured a strong crossface. You're pinned, immobile, and halfway passed.
### Top Half Guard
**Goal:** Pass the guard and move to [[Side Control]] or [[Mount]]
**Keys to passing:**
- Win the underhook battle
- Flatten the bottom player
- Use pressure and good posture
