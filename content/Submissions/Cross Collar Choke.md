## From mount:
1. Start in mount and establish a solid crossface. A key detail: turning their face to the side limits their ability to bridge in multiple directions, making their escapes more predictable.
2. Keep chest pressure over their shoulder, and use your off-hand spread wide on the mat for a strong, low base. Your forehead should also be on the mat to stay heavy.
3. With your crossface arm, establish a **thumb-in grip** on their far-side collar.
4. Circle that arm out to unwind the crossface. As you do, apply **downward elbow pressure on their sternum** while pulling up on the collar to create tension.
5. (Optional) Use your off-hand to adjust and tighten the collar grip—sometimes the initial thumb-in grip can loosen a bit during the unwind.
6. With your off-hand, swoop a big rainbow over their head to the opposite side, staying low and controlled. Do this **quickly**, because during this motion your spine is momentarily aligned with theirs, which compromises your base and makes you vulnerable to being bridged off.
7. Drive your off-hand elbow against their neck, and slide your forearm firmly across their carotid until you can grip behind their shoulder.
8. Square up by shifting your head north of theirs, keeping your forehead posted on the mat.
9. To finish the choke, pull your elbows together and down toward your stomach.
